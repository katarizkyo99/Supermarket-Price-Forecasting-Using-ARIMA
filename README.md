# Supermarket Price Forecasting & Analytics

A time-series forecasting project and interactive dashboard analyzing the price fluctuations of consumer goods across major UK supermarkets. The project utilizes Auto-ARIMA models to predict future price trends, comparing "Own Brand" vs. "National Brand" resilience and identifying price war patterns among top product categories.

[![Streamlit App](https://static.streamlit.io/badges/streamlit_badge_black_white.svg)](https://supermarket-price-forecasting-using-arima-5cnvr5j7qpqijrvxohvt.streamlit.app/)

**Live Demo:** [Supermarket Price Forecasting & Analytics Dashboard)](https://supermarket-price-forecasting-using-arima-5cnvr5j7qpqijrvxohvt.streamlit.app/)

## 📊 Data Source

The raw data used to train the forecasting models is the **[Time Series UK Supermarket Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/declanmcalinden/time-series-uk-supermarket-data)** from Kaggle. 

*Note: To keep this repository lightweight, the raw multi-gigabyte CSV files are not included. Only the processed forecasting results (`Forecast_Ide1_OwnBrand.csv` and `Forecast_Ide2_TopCategories.csv`) generated by the modeling script are pushed to this repository.*

## 🚀 Key Outcomes

* **Automated Time-Series Modeling:** Developed a Python pipeline that iteratively trains `auto_arima` models (incorporating 7-day seasonality) across different supermarkets and product categories.
* **Brand Strategy Analysis:** Segmented data to forecast the price gap and inflation rates between supermarket Own Brands and National Brands.
* **Category Sensitivity:** Analyzed the top 3 staple categories to uncover weekly discounting patterns (seasonality) and projected price hike sensitivity.
* **Interactive Deployment:** Built a Streamlit web application with Plotly to visualize confidence intervals, price margins, and cross-competitor price matching.

## 🧠 Methodology

1. **Data Preprocessing:**
* Concatenated multiple supermarket CSVs into a unified dataset.
* Standardized column names and cast dates to datetime objects.
* Filtered out anomalies (e.g., Morrisons data prior to 2024-01-27) and dropped duplicates.
* Forward-filled (`ffill`) missing daily prices to ensure continuous time-series data.


2. **Auto-ARIMA Forecasting:**
* **Idea 1 (Brand Analysis):** Grouped data by supermarket and brand type. Applied an 80/20 train-test split and forecasted future prices with 95% confidence intervals.
* **Idea 2 (Category Analysis):** Extracted the top 3 highest-volume categories and modeled their price trajectories to observe competitor pricing strategies.


3. **Visualization:**
* Calculated internal inflation percentages based on the first and last predicted values.
* Used box plots to identify specific days of the week prone to price drops.
